## Hi, I'm Pardeep Singh 👋

I build machine learning systems, and the teams that ship them. I'm the Engineering Lead at [Srotas Health](https://www.srotas.health), where we build AI for healthcare: an agentic system that evaluates clinical criteria across 1B+ patient records in under ten seconds, LLM-backed search, and document pipelines for patient feasibility assessment.

Before that I led the ML engineering team at Fairmatic, built conversational AI at Sense, and went from backend to ML engineering at Helpshift. Eleven years in all, the last several on production ML, LLM pipelines and agentic systems.

🌐 **[pardeep-singh.com](https://pardeep-singh.com)** has the full story: experience, projects and what I've been up to lately.
